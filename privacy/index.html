Family Weather

Privacy policy

Last updated October 26, 2026.

Family Weather shows your weather and the weather where your family lives, side by side. It was built so that everything about you and your family stays on your iPhone.

The short version. We don't collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We don't run servers, so nothing you enter is ever sent to us. Your family list, photos, notes and settings live only on your iPhone, and you can erase them at any time in Settings, under Your data.

What leaves your iPhone, and why

  • Weather. To get a forecast, the app sends a location rounded to about 1 km to Apple Weather (WeatherKit). For your own weather that's your current location, if you allow it; for family members it's the city you chose for them. Apple handles these requests under its own privacy policy.
  • Place names and search. When you type a city, or when the app names the city you're in, it asks Apple Maps, again with a rounded location.
  • What you choose to share. Comparison cards, sky postcards, themes, family lists, stickers and story cards go through the app you pick (Messages, Instagram, Facebook, Mail and so on), like sending any photo or file. A shared family list includes names, cities, approximate locations (about 1 km) and time zones, plus birthdays and visits only if you choose to include them. It never includes phone numbers or notes.
  • Purchases. Family Weather Plus is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment, and the app only learns whether Plus is active for your Apple Account. We never see your payment details.

Nothing else leaves your phone.

Permissions

All are optional.

  • Location shows the weather where you are. You can choose your city by hand instead.
  • Notifications tell you when rain is about to start or stop, about severe weather alerts, and other notices you turn on.
  • Camera and Photos are used only when you take or pick a photo for your sky journal or a theme, or save a story card. Journal photos are stored inside the app with their location data removed.
  • Contacts are never read. When you pick a family member's phone number, iOS shows its contact picker and gives the app only the number you tap.
  • Apple Intelligence, on iPhones that have it, can rewrite messages in a warmer or more playful voice, on the iPhone itself.

Children

Family Weather is suitable for all ages and doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.
