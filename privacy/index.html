Family Weather

Privacy policy

Last updated November 11, 2026.

Family Weather shows your weather and the weather where your family lives, side by side, on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. It was built so that everything about you and your family stays on your own devices.

The short version. We don't collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We don't run servers, so nothing you enter is ever sent to us. Your family list, photos, notes and settings live only on your iPhone or iPad, and what the watch app needs is passed straight to your own Apple Watch. You can erase them at any time in Settings, under Your data, and deleting the app from a device removes everything it kept there.

What leaves your device, and why

  • Weather. To get a forecast, the app sends a location rounded to about 1 km to Apple Weather (WeatherKit). For your own weather that's your current location, if you allow it; for family members it's the city you chose for them. Apple handles these requests under its own privacy policy.
  • Past weather in the US and Canada. For places in the United States or Canada, the app asks that country's national weather service for yesterday's and the last week's highs and lows measured at nearby weather stations: the US National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) or Environment and Climate Change Canada (api.weather.gc.ca). It sends only the location rounded to about 1 km, never a name or anything about you. The request also names the app, its version and our contact address, which the National Weather Service asks of every app. Elsewhere, past days come from Apple Weather.
  • Radar. Where the radar screen is offered (most of the United States and Canada), it loads its pictures directly from Environment and Climate Change Canada's weather data service, MSC GeoMet (geo.weather.gc.ca), and only while that screen is open. The app asks for a fixed map square several hundred km across (about 400 to 800 km, depending on how far north it is), shared by everyone in the same area: the square you are in and the squares of the family members shown on the radar, four at most. It never sends your location, exact or rounded. It sends no account, identifier or cookie, and names only the app and its version. Radar pictures and the maps under them are kept for a while in the app's temporary storage on your device, so the screen opens quickly. Erase all data removes them.
  • Place names, search and maps. When you type a city, or when the app names the city you're in, it asks Apple Maps, again with a rounded location. The map under the radar is a picture from Apple Maps of the same map square the radar uses.
  • Apple Watch. If you use the Apple Watch app, your iPhone passes your family list, their forecasts and the settings the watch needs (your theme, units, tone for texts and whether Plus is active) to your own watch, directly from device to device. When your iPhone is out of reach, the watch asks Apple Weather for your own forecast, with the rounded location your iPhone last used. The watch app never reads your location itself, and it has no radar.
  • What you choose to share. Comparison cards, sky postcards, themes, family lists, stickers and story cards go through the app you pick (Messages, Instagram, Facebook, Mail and so on), like sending any photo or file. A shared family list includes names, cities, approximate locations (about 1 km) and time zones, plus birthdays and visits only if you choose to include them. It never includes phone numbers or notes.
  • Purchases. Family Weather Plus is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment, and the app only learns whether Plus is active for your Apple Account. We never see your payment details.

Nothing else leaves your iPhone, iPad or Apple Watch.

Every one of these requests goes straight from your device to the service named, never through us. Like any website you visit, each service sees your internet (IP) address and the usual details of a web request, such as your device's language setting. Apple, the US National Weather Service and Environment and Climate Change Canada handle those under their own privacy policies.

Permissions

All are optional.

  • Location shows the weather where you are. Your exact location stays on your device; only the rounded location or the radar's map square described above is sent. You can choose your city by hand instead.
  • Notifications tell you when rain is about to start or stop, about severe weather alerts, and other notices you turn on.
  • Camera and Photos are used only when you take or pick a photo for your sky journal or a theme, or save a story card. Journal photos are stored inside the app with their location data removed.
  • Contacts are never read. When you pick a family member's phone number, the system shows its contact picker and gives the app only the number you tap.
  • Apple Intelligence, on iPhones and iPads that have it, can rewrite messages in a warmer or more playful voice, on the device itself.

Children

Family Weather is suitable for all ages and doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.
