Common questions

Why don't I see the rain clock?

Minute-by-minute rain comes from Apple Weather and is available only in some countries. Elsewhere the app shows the hourly forecast instead.

How do I add my family?

Open Family and tap Add someone, then choose their city. You can also send everyone a family list from Family, so they can follow the whole family in one tap.

What does unfolding my iPhone do?

On the foldable iPhone, unfolding opens the Family Wall, with a pane for everyone. On other iPhones the Family tab shows the same people as cards.

How do I add widgets?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Family Weather.

How do I turn off the games?

Settings, then Games. Each one switches off on its own.

Family Weather Plus

What does Plus add?

The free app covers you and two people, with the rain clock, the Family Wall, comparisons, postcards, the Messages app and every game. Plus adds everyone else you love, the Our Skies widgets, heads-ups about family, Theme Studio, every app icon and Voices.

Can my family share it?

Yes. Every Plus plan works with Family Sharing, so one purchase covers everyone in your Family Sharing group.

I have a new iPhone. How do I get Plus back?

Open Settings in Family Weather, then Family Weather Plus, and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought Plus with.

How do I cancel?

Yearly and monthly plans renew automatically until you cancel. Cancel in the Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Family Weather. Cancel at least a day before the period or the free week ends. Nothing you've set up is taken away when Plus ends.

How do I ask for a refund?

Apple handles payments, so refunds go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

How do I erase my data?

Settings, then Your data, then Erase all data. It removes your family, forecasts, photos, themes, games and your name from the phone. Read the privacy policy for what the app sends and why.
